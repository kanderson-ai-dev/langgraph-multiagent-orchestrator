The loop

  1. 01

    Supervisor routes

    An LLM supervisor assembles the agent team for the report type, then decides who acts next — no fixed pipeline.

  2. 02

    Researcher gathers

    Real web search and scraping, sanitized against prompt injection.

  3. 03

    Writer ↔ Reviewer debate

    Drafts are graded on a rubric; every quote is verified against the fetched source. Bounded rounds.

  4. 04

    Human in the loop

    If the debate stalls or the budget runs out, it escalates — approve, edit, reject or fund more spend.

  5. 05

    Sealed delivery

    Every report ships with a SHA-256 hash-chained audit trail of the debate that produced it — tamper-evident by construction.
